Privacy Policy

Scrub In for iPhone · Last updated 25 November 2026

Scrub In is a medical-terminology crossword game. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and plain.

Who we are

Scrub In is made by an independent developer, Maria. If you have any question about privacy, email maria@kindrix.io.

What stays on your device

Your solo game progress stays on your iPhone. That covers stars, scores, XP, your streaks, daily check-ins, goals and settings. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the app. We don't ask for your real name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Playing with a friend (2 players)

When you start or join a 2-player match, the app sends the match to our game server so both phones can see the same board. For each match the server stores:

  • a random 5-letter match code, and a random key for each of the two seats, so only those two phones can play the match
  • which puzzle you are playing, the shared board, the letters on each rack, both scores and whose turn it is
  • the time the match was created

The server does not store your name, Apple ID or any device identifier. Anyone you share the code or invite link with can join the free seat. Matches expire 12 hours after they are created and are then deleted. The game server is run on Supabase, a hosting service that stores the data on our behalf.

Usernames and the League (when available)

If you choose a username for the League, the server stores that username, a random player ID and key created on your phone, and your points for each week, so they can appear on the weekly leaderboard. Other players can see your username and weekly points. You can change your username in Settings at any time. To have it deleted, email us.

Downloading new levels and messages

The app downloads new puzzles and Inbox messages from our server. These requests do not include anything about you, apart from the technical information every internet request carries, such as your IP address.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob:

  • Optional rewarded videos: you can choose to watch one for an extra hint or swap.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between puzzles, never during play.

To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out
  • device and app information, such as the model, OS version and language
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostics such as crashes and performance

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and report results, and, where you have agreed, to personalise ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, a consent form from Google asks whether ads may use your data, and for which purposes. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Apple tracking prompt: iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the app does not access your advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads based on context, such as the app itself and your approximate location.

Children

Scrub In is designed for medical and nursing students and other adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data. For 2-player and League data on our server, email us and we will help. For data Google processes for ads, use the choices above or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
