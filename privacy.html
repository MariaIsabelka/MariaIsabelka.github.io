Privacy Policy

Scrub In (MedTerm Break) for iPhone · Last updated 18 November 2026

Scrub In is a medical-terminology crossword game. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Scrub In is made by an independent developer, Maria. If you have any question about privacy, contact maria@kindrix.io.

What stays on your device

Your game progress stays on your iPhone. That covers stars, scores, XP, your daily streak, daily goals and your settings. It is saved only on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the app. We do not run accounts or servers, and we never ask for your name, email address or contacts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear in two ways:

  • Optional rewarded videos. You can choose to watch one to get an extra hint or swap.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between puzzles. They never appear during play.

To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information from your device. This can include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out
  • device and app information, such as the model, OS version and language
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostics such as crashes and performance

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to limit how often you see them, to prevent fraud and to report results. Where you have agreed to it, Google also uses it to personalise ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, a consent form from Google asks whether ads may use your data, and for which purposes. You can change your answer at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Apple tracking prompt: iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the app does not access your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads. These are based on context, such as the app itself and your approximate location.

Children

Scrub In is designed for medical and nursing students and other adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or the CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data. We hold no personal data about you on our side. For data that Google processes for ads, use the choices above, or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. If you have any request, you are also welcome to email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
